Good to know

The short
answers.

Your videos. No hidden catalogue, no cloud library, no confusing monthly plan. More in the help centre.

Does LocalFlix stream or supply movies?

No. It organises and plays video files you already have on your Android phone. There's no catalogue and nothing to download.

Can I watch without internet?

Yes. Your library and player work offline. LocalFlix looks up posters and details automatically when online, and saved artwork remains available offline.

Which formats does it play?

LocalFlix adds MP4, MKV, AVI, MOV, M4V, WebM and TS files. Whether a particular file plays depends on your phone's decoders. If it can't, open it in another player straight from the error screen.

What if a video or folder moves?

Missing videos show as Unavailable and keep their watch history. If a whole folder can't be opened, Reconnect folder points LocalFlix to its new home. You can also link the right video file to any episode yourself.

Does removing something delete it from my phone?

Never. Removing a show, episode or movie only removes it from LocalFlix. The video file stays exactly where it is.

Is Premium a subscription?

No. Free covers up to 10 videos. Premium is a one-time Google Play purchase that removes the limit, and you can restore it after reinstalling.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. LocalFlix is coming to Android first. An iPhone version is planned.
