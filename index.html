Features

Everything a streaming app does, for the files you own.

Movies and shows, sorted for you

LocalFlix reads your filenames, recognises seasons and episodes (and date-stamped episodes too), cleans up the release junk and groups everything into titles.

Posters, cast and details

Add a free TMDB key and LocalFlix fetches posters, backdrops, descriptions and cast. Artwork is saved on your phone, so it still shows offline. Prefer your own? Edit any title by hand.

Continue watching

Every video remembers where you stopped. Pick up mid-episode from the Continue Watching row, and progress bars show how far through each title you are.

Watched, unwatched, in bulk

Mark one episode or a whole selection as watched. Select several episodes to move or remove them together, with Undo if you change your mind.

Search and filters

Search by title, cast member, director, studio or year. Filter by Movies or Shows, Unwatched, In progress, Completed or Has unavailable files, and sort by newest, oldest or A to Z.

Backup and restore

Save your library and watch history to a backup file you control, and restore it later. No cloud account involved.

Standout feature

Point it at a folder. It keeps up.

Add a folder once and LocalFlix looks after it: new episodes, moved files, missing ones and folders that go away and come back.

  • Add folder, including subfolders

    Pick a folder and every video inside it, at any depth, joins your library. Nothing is copied or moved: LocalFlix plays your files where they are.

  • New episodes, found

    Downloaded the next few episodes? Rescan library folders and they slot into the right show and season.

  • Unavailable, not deleted

    If a video goes missing, it is marked Unavailable and keeps its watch history. If it comes back, it plays again. If the whole folder can't be opened, LocalFlix says so and offers Reconnect folder.

  • Link a video file yourself

    For anything LocalFlix can't find on its own, link the right video file to the episode. Folder scans leave manual links alone.

Questions

Good to know.

Is LocalFlix free?

Yes. The free version includes every feature for a library of up to 10 videos. Premium is a one-time purchase that removes the limit. There is no subscription.

Does LocalFlix stream or download films?

No. LocalFlix has no catalogue of its own. It organises and plays video files that are already on your phone.

Which video formats does it play?

LocalFlix adds MP4, MKV, AVI, MOV, M4V, WebM and TS files. Whether a particular file plays depends on what your phone can decode. If it can't, use "Open in another player" to watch it in VLC, MX Player or a similar app.

Do I need a TMDB account?

Only if you want posters and details fetched automatically. TMDB API keys are free. Without one, LocalFlix still sorts and plays everything, and you can add artwork by hand.

What happens if I move or rename a folder?

The affected videos show as Unavailable, with their watch history kept. If the folder itself can't be opened, LocalFlix shows a banner with Reconnect folder: pick the folder's new location and everything is restored.

Does removing something from my library delete the video?

Never. Removing a show, an episode or a movie only removes it from LocalFlix. The video file stays on your phone.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. LocalFlix is on Android first. An iPhone version is planned.

Is Premium a subscription?

No. It is a single purchase through Google Play. If you reinstall, use Restore purchase in Settings.
